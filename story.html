How it started

A small studio, one loom, and a stubborn idea

Gold & Dev began in a two-room studio above a fabric market, where our founder spent evenings unpicking cheap seams from off-the-rack basics and asking why "affordable" always seemed to mean "disposable."

The first collection was eleven pieces, cut and finished by hand, sold directly from a single web page. No investors, no warehouse — just fabric we trusted and customers who noticed the difference.

That same stubbornness is still the whole business model: fewer, better pieces, made by people we know, priced honestly.

Milestones

The journey so far

Six years in, still run by the same principles we opened with — just with a few more hands on deck.

2019

2021

Our first workshop

Demand outgrew the studio. We partnered with a family-run workshop that still cuts every piece we ship today.

2023

We shipped our first international order and never looked back — now worn in more than 120 countries.

2026

Today

A small, deliberately-sized team, one flagship studio, and the same rule we started with: nothing ships that we wouldn't wear ourselves.

How it's made

From raw fabric to finished piece

Three steps, the same three steps every single time — nothing skipped, nothing rushed.

01

Source

We choose fabric mills we've worked with for years, selected for how the cloth ages — not just how it looks off the bolt.

02

Cut & construct

Every pattern is cut and sewn by the same small workshop team, piece by piece, with construction checked at every stage.

03

Finish & fit

A final hand-finish and fitting pass before anything ships — if it wouldn't pass, it doesn't leave the studio.

Our commitment

What we promise, piece by piece

Every decision — fabric, factory, packaging — gets weighed against one question: would we be proud to explain this to a customer? Here's what that looks like in practice.

  • Traceable fabric

    Every textile is sourced from mills we've personally visited and vetted.

  • Fair-wage workshops

    The same small, family-run workshops we started with in 2019, paid fairly and on time.

  • Low-waste packaging

    Recycled mailers, soy-based inks, and zero single-use plastic in every shipment.

  • Repair, not replace

    Free repairs on any Gold & Dev piece for its first three years of wear.
